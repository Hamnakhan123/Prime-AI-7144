Physics Tuition in Karachi

Our physics tuition in Karachi helps O Level and A Level students understand mechanics, electricity, waves and modern physics – and turn that understanding into exam marks. Lessons are one-to-one, at home or online.

What our physics tutors teach

  • O Level physics: measurements, forces, energy, thermal physics, light, electricity and magnetism.
  • A Level physics: kinematics, dynamics, oscillations, fields, quantum physics and practical skills.
  • Paper 3 and 5 practical preparation with real data and uncertainty calculations.

How a physics tuition lesson works

Every lesson begins with a short recap quiz, then a new concept explained with simple experiments or simulations, followed by exam-style questions. Students practise one past-paper question set each week and parents receive a monthly progress report.
