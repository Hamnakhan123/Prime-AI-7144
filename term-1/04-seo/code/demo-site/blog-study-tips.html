10 Study Tips for O & A Level Exams

Exams reward steady practice more than last-minute cramming. These study tips come from tutors who have prepared hundreds of Karachi students for O Level and A Level papers.

1. Plan revision in short, spaced sessions

Study in 40-minute blocks with short breaks, and revisit each topic after one day, one week and one month. Spaced repetition moves knowledge into long-term memory.

2. Practise past papers under timed conditions

Past papers show the style of questions and the marking scheme. Time yourself, then mark honestly using the examiner's report.

3. Use active recall instead of re-reading

Close the book and write everything you remember, or answer flash cards. Retrieval practice is one of the most effective study techniques.

4. Sleep well before the exam

Sleep consolidates memory. A tired brain makes careless mistakes even on topics you know well.
